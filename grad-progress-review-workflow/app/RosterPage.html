Course feedback roster

  1. Get MyGrad registrations. MyGrad opens in a new tab (sign in, or connect to the UW VPN if it does not load). When the student list appears, click the MyGrad Table Audit bookmarklet in that tab and wait for "Sent to the app". Then close the tab.
  2. Get Time Schedule sections. In the Time Schedule tab, click the Time Schedule Sections bookmarklet (it shows only a small purple status box). You type nothing: this page sends it the quarter and the departments. Wait for "Sent to the app", then close the tab. If it asks you for a quarter or curricula, the tab was not opened by this button: cancel, and click the button again.
  3. Check that both lines below the buttons show counts, then click Preview. If the list looks right, click Import.
  4. Add any missing instructor emails, then check the list under "Check before launch". Launch turns on once every page has an email and the opening day (the first day of course feedback) has come.

Instructor pages this quarter

Check before launch

Nothing is emailed until you click Launch. The list below shows exactly what Launch would send: one email to each address, for each page not yet sent.

  1. Addresses. Each address in "Email goes to" gets one email for that course. Check that each one belongs to someone who taught the course, and that the greeting name ("Dear …") is right. Graduate-student instructors are left out on purpose.
  2. Students. Check that the students listed are the Anthropology graduate students you expect in that course. "advisee" means an instructor on that page is the student's advisor (from Advisor_Emails in Students at the last import), so that instructor can answer "Covered in my annual review".
  3. Left out. The line under the table counts the registrations left out of every page, and why. A page with no students closes at launch and sends nothing.
  4. The email. Open "The email instructors receive" to read the text exactly as sent, with the first page's details.
  5. Fixing a wrong or extra address. Saving and importing only add addresses, so remove or correct it in the spreadsheet: the Instructor_Emails column of that course's row in Feedback_Requests, and the Email column in Instructors so the next import uses it. Then click Refresh the list.
  6. Wrong students or a missing course. Get the MyGrad and Time Schedule data again, then Preview and Import. Import replaces the quarter's student list and keeps the pages.

Annual review: MyGrad data

Milestones, leave, GPA, candidacy, ANTH 800 credits and the year's courses for the self-report (annual review plan §9.3). Students confirm or correct every value.

Click Get MyGrad data. In the MyGrad tab that opens, click the MyGrad Table Audit bookmarklet and wait for "Sent to the app". Then close the tab.
