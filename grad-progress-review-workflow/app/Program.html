UW Anthropology · Graduate Student Annual Review (Policy 3.7)

Program Stage and Calibration

For the GPC, or the alternate reviewer for the GPC's own advisees.

Open each calibration meeting with the rating levels , using one or two anonymized examples from past cycles; in the first cycle, write the examples yourself.
  • Satisfactory: meeting expectations.
  • Satisfactory with concerns: on track overall, with specific issues.
  • Unsatisfactory: not meeting performance or progress expectations.

Course feedback appears as a summary only. Its rating levels are never compared across instructors or students.
