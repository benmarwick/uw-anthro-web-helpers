Review and release

Each quarter's comments, follow-up conversations, and the yearly Course Feedback Records.

Before releasing each comment, check:
  1. Is anything about the person rather than the work?
  2. Is anything outside course work (grades, TA work, health)?
  3. Is any comment unclear?
  4. Is anything faint praise or hedged, so that the student could not tell what the instructor means?

You never edit an instructor's words. Return a comment with a reason instead; the instructor has 5 days to revise. A comment still unrevised on release day goes to the student only, with your note.
