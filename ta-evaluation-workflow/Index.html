Instructions

The Graduate School requires that new Academic Student Employees (ASEs, including TAs, RAs and predoctoral instructors) be evaluated during their first two ASE assignments. Anthropology has generally gone further, expecting an evaluation every quarter for every ASE position. This form is to be completed by the instructor. The instructor must discuss and review their responses to this form together with the ASE who is the subject of the evaluation.

Per Graduate School Policy 5.1: Departmental Responsibilities Regarding Instruction by TAs, criteria for teaching competence and procedures for observations are to be determined by each department and are to be communicated to the ASE prior to the observation, at the beginning of the quarter.

In the Anthropology Department, we have documented some expectations in 'Working Together for Student Success: A Guide for Faculty and TAs in the UW Anthropology Department'. Departmental evaluations are generally distributed during the 7th week of each quarter. Contact agp-advisor@uw.edu if you wish to evaluate your appointee prior to that time.

Guidelines for these evaluations are governed by Graduate School Policy 5.1 and Executive Order 28: Graduate Student Service Appointments. Please review these before completing your review.

Responses to this form will be included in the official personnel file of the academic student employee (cf. article 22 of the contract) which ASEs have the right to examine and request corrections or respond.
